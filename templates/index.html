Get pdforge for your computer

The free pdforge app runs on Mac, Windows and Linux computers. On a computer you can work with PDFs of any size.

Open pdforge-nms.vercel.app on your computer and click Download app.

  • No file size limit: work with PDFs of any size
  • Private: your files never leave your computer
  • Works offline, free, and only about 10 MB
macOSApple Silicon (M1 and newer) macOSIntel Windows10 and 11 (64-bit) LinuxUbuntu / Debian (.deb)
First time opening it?

The app isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so your computer asks once:

macOS: open the downloaded file, drag pdforge to Applications, then right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again.

Windows: if you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
